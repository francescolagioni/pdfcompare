Support

Write to me

pdfcompare.support@gmail.com — replies usually within two working days.

It helps if you say which version of macOS you are on and what the two documents are like: scanned or digital, how many pages, whether the page size changed. You do not need to send the documents, and please do not send anything confidential.

Common questions

Are my documents uploaded anywhere?

No. The app has no network access at all: networking is switched off in its sandbox, so it cannot send anything even in principle. See the privacy page.

What is free and what is paid?

Documents up to 20 pages are compared in full and free, with no time limit and no account. Above 20 pages the comparison still runs completely and every changed page is counted and listed, but the document is shown down to the fifth changed page and covered after it; exported reports stop at the same point and say so. There is no watermark anywhere. The one-time unlock shows the rest. There is no subscription.

I bought it and it is asking me to buy again

Choose Restore Purchases, in the unlock panel or in the app menu. Make sure you are signed in to the Mac App Store with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Can I use it on my other Mac?

Yes. The purchase is tied to your Apple Account, not to one computer.

It says a page is entirely different but it looks the same

Usually the page size changed between versions, or the page was re-scanned and has no text layer, so it is compared visually rather than textually. The overlay view normally shows which. If neither explains it, write to me and tell me what the two documents are.

Can it compare a PDF with a Word file?

No, two PDFs only. Export the Word file to PDF first.

Does it do OCR?

No. Scanned pages are compared as images, which finds changes but cannot quote the text.

Can it open a password-protected PDF?

No. Remove the password first, then compare.

Is there a version for iPad?

Not yet. The app is macOS only for now.

I want a refund

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by me — go to reportaproblem.apple.com. Tell me what went wrong as well, so I can fix it.

Reporting something that is wrong

A comparison tool that misses a change is worse than useless. If you find one it missed, or a difference it reported that isn't real, that is the most valuable thing you can send me. A description of the situation is enough — I do not need your files.
