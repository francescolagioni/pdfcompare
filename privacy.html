Privacy

Nothing leaves your Mac

The app collects nothing, sends nothing and stores nothing about you. This page exists because Apple requires one, not because there is much to disclose.

The app

PDF Compare Offline runs in Apple's sandbox with both incoming and outgoing network access switched off. It cannot connect to the internet, so your documents cannot leave your Mac through it — by design rather than by promise.

There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising. The app reads the files you open and their modification dates, which it uses to suggest which of the two is the newer version. It writes only where you tell it to: the report you export, in the folder you chose.

The purchase

The one-time unlock is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never see your name, your email address or your payment details. The app asks Apple whether this Apple Account owns the unlock, and Apple answers yes or no.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They load no fonts, scripts, trackers or images from other companies, and set no cookies. GitHub records server logs, including IP addresses, as any web host does; that is covered by GitHub's privacy statement.

Email

If you write to support, I keep the message for as long as it takes to answer it and, where it describes a bug, until that bug is fixed. I do not add you to anything and there is nothing to unsubscribe from.
